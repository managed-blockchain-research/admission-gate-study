## Nethermind heap sensitivity (attack-only burst; n = 4 per cell; 2,048 MiB = mixed_zgc_sustained_pulse block N, n = 5, different batch, shown only beside)
- 1,024 MiB (H1, 1,200 attack tx): node died in 4/4 none runs (114-172 s after start), 4/4 SizeStatic runs (116-150 s; attack block 1.0) and 2/4 PressureOnly runs (110 and 156 s; attack block 0.37, benign rejected 408-8,535 per run). Low-intensity H1L (400 attack tx): 4/4 none and 4/4 SizeStatic died (108-138 s). Peak RSS about 1,350 MB at a 1,024 MiB limit. Benign-only control H1B (4 runs, no burst, node cpu08 because cpu05 was occupied; PREREG heap_sweep_and_sampling section 8-9): the node died in 4 of 4 runs at 108, 114, 126 and 130 s after the start, i.e. inside the 240 s load, with no attack at all (peak RSS 1,338-1,368 MB, 2-29 OOM lines per run). So at 1,024 MiB this ordinary benign workload alone is not sustained: the deaths seen in H1/H1L cannot be attributed to the attack, and the defenses (SizeStatic, PressureOnly) did not prevent them. Death times in the attack runs (108-172 s) overlap those of the benign-only runs (108-130 s); no later-than-benign delay is claimed.
- 3,072 and 4,096 MiB (H3, H4, H3L, H4L): no OOM, no death in any arm. PressureOnly attack block 0.000 and benign rejected 0 (the pressure signal did not reach its ramp), receipts equal to none (3,072: 13,211 vs 13,196; 4,096: 13,098 vs 13,048); SizeStatic +4,360 / +4,508 receipts over none (H3/H4), +1,578 / +1,602 (H3L/H4L). Peak RSS 3,380-3,478 MB at the 3,072 MiB limit (RSS includes memory outside the managed heap; it is not read as heap-limit compliance).
