## Reading in one line
Deferral converts rejections into late deliveries; it only helps legitimate heavy traffic when the offered heavy rate is close to the allowed rate (D3R); under a large overload (D1R/D2R) most of what is deferred is delivered after the 120 s deadline or dropped at the delay cap, and the node sees almost the same extra load as with the larger allowance.
